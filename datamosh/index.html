Datamosher

Real datamoshing, not a pixel-shuffling filter: your video is re-encoded to VP8 with WebCodecs, then the compressed bitstream is broken on purpose — keyframes ripped out so scene cuts melt, P-frames duplicated so motion blooms and smears. Works on a video file or a live webcam.

Everything runs in your browser. Your video and camera never leave this device — there is no server to send them to.

Your browser doesn’t support VP8 encoding via WebCodecs, which this tool needs for bitstream-level moshing. Try a Chromium-based browser (Chrome, Edge, Arc, Brave…) or a recent Firefox.

Mosh parameters — re-applied to the bitstream live, no re-encode

Moshed .webm is the corrupted bitstream itself — it glitches (differently!) in every player. Bake replays the mosh once and records the canvas, freezing exactly what you see here into a clean file.

After one initial keyframe the encoder is never allowed another, so the picture accumulates drift and smear on its own. Hold Bloom to re-apply the last P-frame’s motion over and over; hold Drop to discard frames so reality jumps when you release; Resync lets a single keyframe through to snap back to clean video.
